# Editorial brief · from a broad question to a testable comparison

Original question: **How is AI adoption changing Dutch businesses, and what could that mean for banks such as ABN AMRO?**

Working question: how does reported AI use differ by company size in the published Dutch 2024 survey, and how does the interpretation change when the denominator is turnover rather than firms?

Assumptions: English, informed business reader, about 800–1,000 words, explanatory rather than promotional. AURORA's actual local January 2026 guide informs plain, active British English. No current bank commission or approval is claimed.

Why narrow: CBS offers six compatible size groups for one year. A same-year comparison avoids mixing the 2023 retail survey, the 2026 cyber survey and different AI definitions into an artificial time trend. The topic still informs questions about implementation support and how a bank might assess a business plan. It cannot establish business outcomes or loan quality.
