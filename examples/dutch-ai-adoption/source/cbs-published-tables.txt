L41: # 2 .Use of AI technology by Dutch companies
L42: 
L43: This chapter examines the use of AI technologies by Dutch companies with ten or more employees.
L44: 
L45: Questions on use of AI technology in survey on ICT use in enterprises
L46: The data used for this chapter were taken from the cite34†survey on ICT usage in enterprises. This survey describes ICT use among firms with at least one person employed in sections C through N and Q of the NACE classification of economic activities. Since 2020, it has included a series of questions about the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI), which are posed to companies with ten or more employees.
L47: They ask whether companies use specific AI technologies, for what purposes these AI technologies are used, and how the AI technologies were obtained. Companies indicating that they do not use AI are asked if they have ever considered using such technology, and if not, for what reason. There is some variation over the years in the questions asked, as well as the response categories used. A summary of these questions and categories can be found in cite30†Appendix 1 .
L48: For the period 2021-2024, the use of at least one of the following seven AI technologies could be identified consistently: machine learning, service robots or autonomous vehicles, robot-assisted process automation, speech recognition, image recognition, text mining and natural language generation. However, a consistent time series from 2020 onwards could only be established for the first four of the listed technologies.

L244: ## 2.2 Revenue share of companies with AI technology
L245: Companies that used AI technology accounted for more than half (51.1 percent) of the total turnover in 2024 by companies with ten or more employees in sectors C through N and Q; companies that did not use AI accounted for the remaining 48.9 percent of turnover. The revenue share of companies that used AI technology was greater among larger companies.
L246: For example, among companies with ten to nineteen employees, those that used AI technology accounted for 18.7 percent of turnover, compared with 79.5 percent for companies with 500 or more employees. This positive relationship between revenue share and company size can be partly explained by the fact that larger companies are more likely to use AI technology.
