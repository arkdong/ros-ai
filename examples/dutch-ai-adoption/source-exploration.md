1. **CBS, Dutch AI monitor 2024, chapter 2**, English publication 18 June 2025, retrieved 23 September 2026. https://www.cbs.nl/en-gb/longread/aanvullende-statistische-diensten/2025/ai-monitor-2024/2-use-of-ai-technology-by-dutch-companies
   Primary aggregate statistical evidence. Tables 2.1.3 and 2.2.1 publish six compatible size groups for 2024. Adopted direction: compare size groups and challenge the denominator. Population: enterprises with at least ten persons employed in NACE C–N and Q. Estimates are provisional. The chart values and published intervals are captured in `source/cbs-published-tables.txt`; exact extraction code and hashes are retained. These are not firm-level observations. No subgroup microbusiness, productivity or causal inference is possible.

2. **ABN AMRO Insights, Mkb niet klaar voor nieuwe digitale risico's**, Julia Krauwer, 23 June 2026, retrieved 23 September 2026. https://www.abnamro.nl/nl/zakelijk/insights/sectoren-en-trends/alle-sectoren/mkb-niet-klaar-voor-nieuwe-digitale-risicos.html
   Attributed contemporary banking context. The article reports ABN AMRO/MWM2 research among 777 Dutch organisations and discusses smaller firms' preparedness for digital risks and AI governance. It motivates asking about implementation controls alongside use. Its sample, revenue-based SME classification and technology questions differ from CBS; its percentages are not joined to the CBS table, plotted as a trend or treated as bank-client outcomes. The underlying microdata were not obtained.

Useful for framing an implementation question; too narrow and differently sampled to establish nationwide adoption. No numeric claim from it enters the calculations.
