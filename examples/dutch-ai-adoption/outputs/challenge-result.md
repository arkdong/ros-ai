CHALLENGE CHANGES THE DRAFT. In the 500+ group, AI users are 59.2% of enterprises and account for 79.5% of turnover, a 20.3-point difference between different denominators. This does not mean AI generated 79.5% of sales. The turnover share is slightly below the firm share in two size bins (20–49 and 50–99), so revenue overrepresentation is not universal within size groups. Businesses below ten persons employed are absent, not zero adopters. Replace the broad claim about all small Dutch firms or AI value creation with: within the surveyed sectors and 10+ population, reported AI use rises with company size; aggregate adoption and turnover associations do not establish productivity, profitability, causality or the position of microbusinesses. Banking applications remain hypotheses requiring client-specific evidence.
