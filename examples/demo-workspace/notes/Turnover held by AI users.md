> among companies with ten to nineteen employees, those that used AI technology accounted for 18.7 percent of turnover, compared with 79.5 percent for companies with 500 or more employees.

**Why it matters:** the share of turnover held by AI users rises steeply with company size, the same pattern as AI use itself.
