ABN AMRO's June 2026 Insights article reports research with MWM2 among 777 Dutch organisations on digital risks and AI; its title says it plainly: smaller businesses are not ready for new digital risks [1]. It is a separate survey with its own definitions, so it is useful context for what a bank could ask, not extra data to combine with CBS [1].
