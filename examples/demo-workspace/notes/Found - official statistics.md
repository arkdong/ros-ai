CBS publishes AI use for six company-size groups in 2024, from 17.8% of businesses with 10–19 employees to 59.2% of those with 500 or more [1]. It also gives the share of turnover that belongs to businesses using AI, by the same groups [1]. The figures are provisional, cover businesses with ten or more employees in sectors C–N and Q, and leave out smaller firms [1].
