A short guide for reading what the assistant reports.

- **Average and median.** The average adds everything up; the median is the middle day. If they differ a lot, a few extreme days drive the average.
- **How many days?** A difference between 104 weekend days and 262 weekdays can be chance. Ask how big it is compared with the normal variation from day to day.
- **Does it hold everywhere?** A pattern for the whole year can disappear or reverse within a season. Ask the follow-up.
- **One year is one year.** A pattern in 2024 is not a law of nature.
