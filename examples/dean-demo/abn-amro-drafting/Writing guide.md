A short summary of the ABN AMRO Writing Guide (January 2026, version 1.1) as the AURORA drafting project applied it. It guides style only; it is not evidence.

- Put the main point first. The opening paragraph says what the reader learns.
- Know the reader and the purpose before writing.
- Use short, active sentences and familiar words.
- One subject per paragraph.
- Make headings concrete: they say what the section shows.
- Write in British English.
- Tell the story honestly. No claim without a source, no hype.
- Close with a practical next step for the reader, without promoting a product.
- Link each source where it is used.
