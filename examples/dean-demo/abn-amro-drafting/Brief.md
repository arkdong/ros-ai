**Question:** How is AI adoption changing Dutch small and medium-sized businesses, and what should a bank such as ABN AMRO ask them about it?

**Output:** a draft article in the style of ABN AMRO Insights, about 700–900 words, in British English. Read by business owners and by the relationship managers who talk to them.

**Evidence rules**
- Every factual sentence in the draft comes from a Finding note that quotes its source word for word.
- Prefer primary sources: CBS, KVK, De Nederlandsche Bank, the European Commission, OECD. ABN AMRO's own Insights articles are context, not proof.
- Use Dutch and English sources.
- Surveys differ in who they ask (firm size, sector, year). Never join their percentages into one trend.
- Keep what a source measured apart from what we infer from it.

**Out of scope:** product promotion, lending advice and claims about ABN AMRO's own clients.

**Sign-off:** nothing is published. The draft is saved only when the researcher accepts it, and it goes through human editorial review after that.
