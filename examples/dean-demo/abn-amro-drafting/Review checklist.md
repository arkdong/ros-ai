Checks taken from the AURORA drafting pipeline (citations, audit trace, quality checks, human sign-off). The researcher goes through them before accepting the draft.

1. **Main point first.** Does the opening say what the reader learns?
2. **Every claim is traceable.** Each factual sentence links to a source, and that source has a Finding note quoting it word for word.
3. **Numbers keep their context.** Each figure names its source, year and who was surveyed.
4. **No false trends.** Different surveys are not joined into one series.
5. **Limits are stated.** The draft says what the evidence does not show.
6. **House style.** Short active sentences, concrete headings, British English.
7. **No promotion or advice.** No products, no lending advice, no claims about ABN AMRO clients.
8. **Human sign-off.** Accepting saves the draft as a new report version; it does not publish it.
