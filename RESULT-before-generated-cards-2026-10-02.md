## Delivered

- Multiple clarifiers now collapse to **Answer questions**. The popup gives each question its own explicit choices (select up to two), custom text, and **Not important · leave out**. Unanswered questions remain optional. Choices come from model-provided structured fields or recognizable examples in historical wording; ordinary questions receive no invented choices.
- Answer drafts persist in this browser separately by project, turn and exact question/choice content. Closing, Escape and reload preserve them. **Add answers to draft** appends readable answers without replacing the unfinished message or sending inference. The combined message is checked against the existing 12,000-character limit; an oversized message keeps both drafts and shows an error. Custom answers allow 2,000 characters each. Keyboard focus stays in the dialog, and its question area scrolls with the footer visible on phones.
- Card outlines now have 26 px indentation and branch lines. Knowledge uses blue square book symbols; Actions use amber round flask symbols. Text labels remain visible. Groups retain their own symbol. Invalid parents and cycles—including existing saved ancestry—remain warnings. Recovered outlines still grant no actions and remain **Draft · not applied**.
- Header **Add** and **New report** are removed. Add block, Undo/Redo, content History and tutorial controls belong to the canvas. Reports remain under **Add block → Report**; the empty canvas offers **Add your first block** and Groups keep **Add inside**. Project permissions, budget, resources and Connections stay in the project header.
- The header toggle and panel title are **Assistant**. The canvas is pure white with clearer gray dots, 1.4 px at a 24 px grid matching snapping.
