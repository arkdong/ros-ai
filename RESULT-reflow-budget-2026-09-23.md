## Limits and untested edges

Large groups can extend beyond the viewport; pan/zoom remains intentional rather than an automatic fit. Non-overlap checks concern settled frames, not every intermediate animation frame. Reflow preserves the user's spatial order rather than continuously rebuilding a globally optimal graph layout. Deep nesting, extreme canvas sizes and a full mobile matrix were not exhaustively browser-tested; nesting/cycles have focused helper coverage. The no-project title fallback and future rename-data propagation are implemented but were not separately exercised in the browser. No new project-rename UI was introduced.

The existing >500KB Vite bundle advisory remains. Nebius remote execution remains blocked pending verified pricing/free-beta terms; this update neither authorizes nor performs remote execution. All paid admissions remain disabled in the handoff server.
