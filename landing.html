Yours, on your own machine.

ros.ai is open source under MIT. It runs on your computer and talks only to the model and search providers you connect.

  • Served on 127.0.0.1. Nothing listens beyond your machine.
  • Keys stay server-side. The browser never sees them.
  • Spending limits per agent. Agents pause when a budget runs out.
  • Local inference. Ros and Ollama models never leave your laptop.

Common questions

Which agents can I use?

Ros runs locally. Claude Code and Codex connect with your own keys or logins, and any agent that speaks MCP can join through the ros_* tools.

Does my data leave my computer?

Only the requests you send to providers you connect. Projects, keys and the sandbox stay on your machine.

What do I need to run it?

Node 24 and Docker Desktop. Ollama if you want Ros or other local models.

Is it really free?

Yes. The app is MIT licensed. Organisations pay only if they want us to host it.
